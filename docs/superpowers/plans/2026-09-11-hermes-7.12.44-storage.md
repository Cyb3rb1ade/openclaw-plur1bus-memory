history/scopes under the writer lock. Use a per-agent, canonical-UUID receipt
with scope/session/source hashes, original capturedAt, deterministic record IDs
and bounded append offsets/fingerprints. Persist prepared intent atomically
before appending, validate all existing journal and episode evidence before any
write, and commit the receipt last. Replay must recover actual append-then-crash
states, including another capture appending before retry, or clearly fail closed
without overwriting unrelated data. Receipt/file drift or truncation must not be
silently certified. Reuse the durable timestamp when captured_at is omitted.

authorization. Dead-letter filtering must independently retain foreign entries
even when their string-coerced retry keys collide with an owned entry.
The identity guarantee begins when a legacy retry receives a persisted ID; old
random-ID history is never deduplicated by text. Document supported recovery and
non-transactional side effects in a tracked audit, not only the SDD ledger.
